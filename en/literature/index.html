Bibliography

The textbooks the course follows, and then every work each lecture cites — books with the chapter or section the lecture uses, and the original papers.

Books and course notes

The per-lecture References sections cite the works below by author and short title, with printed pages. Several use conventions different from this course’s (qubit order, Grover’s angle, the reading direction of diagrams); the lectures translate at the point of use (see the Notation Bible).

  • M. A. Nielsen and I. L. Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, 10th anniversary ed. Cambridge University Press, 2010.

  • R. de Wolf, Quantum Computing: Lecture Notes, version 6. arXiv:1907.09415, 2026.

  • J. Watrous, Understanding Quantum Information and Computation. arXiv:2507.11536, 2025.

  • A. Yu. Kitaev, A. H. Shen and M. N. Vyalyi, Classical and Quantum Computation. AMS (GSM 47), 2002.

  • A. Kissinger and J. van de Wetering, Picturing Quantum Software, version 1.3.0, 2026.

  • D. Gottesman, Stabilizer Codes and Quantum Error Correction. PhD thesis, Caltech, 1997. arXiv:quant-ph/9705052.

  • A. Yu. Kitaev, Fault-tolerant quantum computation by anyons. arXiv:quant-ph/9707021, 1997.

  • Quantum Mechanics I: Finite Systems (QM-I), the author’s course notes. Lectures 1, 3–4 and 10–11 supply the finite-system background; Lecture 9 fixes the discrete Fourier convention.

  • Quantum Mechanics II: Continuous Systems (QM-II), the author’s course notes. Lectures 4 and 7 treat the continuous Fourier transform and Hamiltonian evolution.

  • Quantum Mechanics III: Foundations (QM-III), the author’s course in development under design draft 0.4 (Lecture 1 written). Lectures 7 and 9–10, on channels and measurement, remain planned further study; they are not prerequisites.
